About GBENE OGONI
For The Next Generation

The first digital dictionary + voice dataset for Ogoni speakers. Built by Ogoni, for Ogoni.

Who We Are

We are dedicated to the preservation, promotion, and digital documentation of Ogoni languages — starting with Khana (Kana), the most widely spoken language of the Ogoni people in Rivers State, Nigeria.

Ogoni language is more than words. It is our identity, our history, our proverbs, our songs, and the voice of our elders. But today, many of our children can understand Ogoni but cannot speak or read it fluently. Our language is classified as vulnerable, and if we do nothing, a great part of who we are will be lost.

This website was created to make sure that never happens.

We are building a living, searchable, and growing digital dictionary and learning platform for Ogoni languages — with authentic words sourced from elders, linguistic works like Roger Blench's research, and community contributions.

Our Mission: To make every Ogoni child, home and abroad, able to learn, speak, read, and be proud of their language using modern technology.

Our Vision: To become the number one digital home for all Ogoni languages — Khana, Gokana, Eleme, and Tai — accessible to the world.

Why This Project Matters - The Reasons

  • 1. Language is Dying: UNESCO estimates that an indigenous language dies every 2 weeks. Ogoni youth in Port Harcourt, Lagos, and in diaspora are fast losing fluency. We must act now.
  • 2. No Central Resource: Before now, there was no single, easy-to-use online place to learn Ogoni words. Learners had to search scattered PDFs and old books. We are changing that.
  • 3. Bridging Elders and Youth: Our elders are custodians of deep knowledge. This platform connects their wisdom to a generation that lives on phones and laptops.
  • 4. Cultural Identity: You cannot truly be Ogoni without your language. When you lose your language, you lose your proverbs (alee), your stories, your worldview.

For Children & Students

Learn your mother tongue with simple English to Ogoni translations, pronunciations, and numbers (Koo, Baa, Taa).

For Parents in Diaspora

Teach your children at home in UK, US, Canada how to speak Ogoni, even when you are far from Bori.

For Researchers & Linguists

Get access to verified 72+ words, proto-Ogoni roots, and sources for academic work.

For The Ogoni Nation

It strengthens unity, preserves heritage, and shows the world that Ogoni culture is alive and digital.

For Tourism & Pride

A language alive online makes Ogoni land more visible and culturally respected globally.

Why This Is A Welcome Idea

This project is welcome because it is timely, necessary, and owned by the people.

It is not just another website — it is a rescue mission. At a time when many Nigerian languages are disappearing, choosing to preserve Ogoni is an act of resistance and love.

It welcomes every Ogoni son and daughter to contribute, to correct, to add a word they heard from their grandmother, and to feel that their identity matters in the digital age.

It tells our children: Your language is not inferior. It is good enough to be on Google, good enough to be an app, good enough for the future.

In short: This is the digital ark for the Ogoni voice.
